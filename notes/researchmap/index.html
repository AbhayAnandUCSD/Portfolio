Research Map

Oct 2026

A lot of people have been asking me about how to ‘get into research’. I’d say, generally the most important thing you could do is figure out what you like. Research should be fun, and ideally you should be really excited to share your research.

I’ve made a little tool here to help anyone interested in ML research and is a little confused. I've scraped papers from some of the most popular conferences.

I’d suggest filtering by the topics you like at a general level (for examples, computer vision, robotics etc.) and then reading a bunch of abstracts (use learn mode once you've applied your filters). Then once you have a good idea of what the current research directions are you can center into a more specific niche (like meta learning, or latent space steering).

Lastly, search for professors at your school who work on these topics and reach out to them (and their graduate students). Keep those brief, and ideally attach a specific area or project you’d want to explore.

Hope this helps anyone starting their journey.
